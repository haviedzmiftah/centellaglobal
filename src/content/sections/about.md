Centella Global is a multilingual data services provider specializing in translation, transcription, localization, voice recording, voice-over, script writing, social media evaluation, content creation, and social media management. We deliver AI-ready data solutions and custom datasets tailored to our clients' needs. Our operations in Indonesia began in 2019 and PT Centella Global Corp was formally established in 2025.
