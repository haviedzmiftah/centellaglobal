# About Centella Global

**PT Centella Global Corp** is a multilingual data services provider specializing in translation, transcription, localization, voice recording, voice-over, script writing, social media evaluation, content creation, and social media management. We deliver comprehensive AI-ready data solutions and custom datasets tailored to our clients' unique needs.

With operations beginning in Indonesia in 2019 and formal establishment of PT Centella Global Corp in 2025, we have expanded internationally through **Centella Global Danışmanlık Ltd. Şti.** in Turkey, serving clients across the Middle East and European markets.

## Our Vision

To be a trusted, inclusive, and highly competitive company in our field, delivering value to customers, business partners, and society.

## Our Mission

1. Deliver high-quality, professional services with a strong focus on customer satisfaction.
2. Create meaningful work opportunities through flexible, home-based arrangements, enabling contributors from diverse circumstances to participate and thrive.
3. Foster a sustainable collaboration ecosystem with freelancers, professionals, and business partners.
4. Develop human resources and leverage technology to drive business growth.

## Our Services

Centella Global offers a comprehensive range of services:

- **Language & Localization**: Translation, Transcription, Localization, Subtitling, Proofreading & Editing
- **Audio & Voice**: Voice Recording, Voice-over Production, Audio Transcription & Annotation
- **Content Services**: Script Writing, Social Media Evaluation, Social Media Management, Content Creation
- **Data Services & AI Support**: Custom Dataset Creation, AI-ready Data Solutions, Data Labeling & Annotation
