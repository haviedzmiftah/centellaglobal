About Centella Global

Centella Global is a multilingual data services provider specializing in translation, transcription, localization, voice recording, voice-over, script writing, social media evaluation, content creation, and social media management. We also have the capability to develop custom datasets and AI-ready data solutions upon client request. While our operations in Indonesia began in 2019, PT Centella Global Corp was formally established in 2025. We also operate through Centella Global Danışmanlık Ltd. Şti. in Turkey, with projects targeting the Middle East and European markets managed through the Turkey entity to streamline local operations and compliance.

Our Services

Language & Localization Services

We bridge communication gaps through comprehensive linguistic solutions, including precise translation and transcription that preserve your message's original intent. Beyond literal meaning, our localization and subtitling services adapt your content culturally and technically, ensuring it resonates deeply with global audiences and meets the specific demands of any local market.

Audio & Voice Services

Our audio suite provides end-to-end solutions for sound-based media, ranging from high-fidelity voice recording to polished voice-over production for diverse visual projects. We also specialize in audio transcription and annotation, converting spoken words into structured data while identifying key auditory elements to support both creative projects and technical analysis.

Content Services

We drive brand engagement through a full spectrum of creative content, from professional script writing to the production of high-impact text, visuals, and multimedia assets. To ensure maximum reach, we offer social media evaluation and full-scale management, optimizing your digital presence and maintaining a consistent, compelling voice across all social platforms.

Data Services & AI Support

We empower the future of technology by providing AI-ready data solutions and custom dataset creation tailored to specific client needs. Our expertise includes meticulous data labeling and annotation across video, audio, text, and image formats, complemented by professional proofreading and editing to ensure the highest standards of data integrity and linguistic accuracy.
